# PDF072 membership repair: V4 with bounded review

This revision follows the failed V2 scheduler study and failed V3 controlled review, whose outcomes
remain preserved. V4 replaces the longer clarification with short affirmative criteria: include the
title lines of main and self-contained embedded documents; include standalone labels that introduce
the following table/schedule/content unit; judge dates, venues, addresses and formats separately as
possible attributes. Membership is independent of outline parents. No source alias or document name
is in the prompt. The underlying structural instruction remains present.

V3's reviewer sometimes paraphrased its instruction as excluding cover blocks, even though that was
not the requested rule. That visible explanation is diagnostic self-report, not a verified causal
trace. V4 tests wording as a new revision; its successes cannot retroactively qualify V2 or V3.

The code mechanism stays the same as V3: source-bound V1, at most two complete scopes reviewed,
at most two conversation posts per scope, complete valid replacement only, and original decisions
retained on invalid or incomplete review. The routing uses first complete scope and one scope with
largest measured font size; it does not classify headings. Source context and prior selected lines
are frozen before concurrent review. No parser keyword or automatic Gold-based restoration is added.

The controlled fixtures and predeclared gates are identical to V3. Both frozen PDF072 reviews must
recover all three missed agenda aliases and reduce existing cover false positives. Both PDF076
first-page reviews must recover its two missing title aliases without adding a false positive. The
gate precedes two full PDF072 samples. The experiment plan and complete failed predecessors are
stored alongside the source identity; no unchanged revision is rerun selectively until passing.

The concurrency finding is separate: all 650 aliases are owned once and collected faithfully; the
three-alias omission also occurs during serial inference. Local parallel aggregation is not the
mechanism of those audited omissions. Neither that finding nor a small live study rules out hidden
provider-load effects on inference or establishes a general p95 time guarantee.

Artifacts: `artifacts/qualification/pdf072-membership-v4-review-20261024/` with 316 snapshotted code
files, isolated builds, controlled request/reply traces, scores and any subsequent full runs.

## Result

V4 is rejected. Both frozen PDF072 repetitions recover all 44 Gold aliases, but false positives are
7 and 3 respectively, versus 3 in the initial selection. The first repetition adds four descriptive
agenda-header lines. The second keeps the original three cover false positives. Neither passes the
predeclared strict-reduction criterion; the first also regresses precision. Review coverage and protocol
checks pass in both. Times are 69.826 and 44.683 seconds, not end-to-end extraction times.

All 316 snapshotted source files match before the next revision. 245 regression tests pass. The two
PDF076 calls were not made: the conjunctive controlled gate already fails on PDF072, and an independent
result cannot change that outcome. Full extraction was not run. No favorable V4 sample is substituted
for the failed first repetition.

The next revision keeps the recovered local-title/label criterion and explicitly separates a unit's
attributes from the content unit itself. Its source identity, plan and outcomes are stored separately.
