## Known operational limitations

- `dhx.cmd` selects `out-vulkan` before `bin/Release` and runs the first binary that exists, not the
  newest. At the time of this record `out-vulkan` was 403 commits behind. Confirm the executed
  binary's revision, or invoke the intended one directly.
- `--openrouter` is required; without it the CLI silently selects the Local backend and succeeds. The
  artifact reports `backend: Local` honestly, and such a run is not OpenRouter evidence.
- 25 of 28 emitted headings carry no resolved level, so a writeback skips them as `level_unresolved`.
  That is the contract, not a fault.
- `validatedHeadings: 0` is not by itself a failure — read `semanticLaneStatus` first, and treat a
  null status as unknown rather than complete.
