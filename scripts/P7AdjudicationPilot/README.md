Provider-free, qualification-only. Fixed six whole pages across five already frozen source documents;
selected from source-form observations, not provider successes/failures. The reference PDF is known
development data. No held-out/generalization claim. Existing shape renders are reused, not extended.

The executable verifies pinned original PDF/snapshot/render hashes and writes new files only.
It exports **every** occurrence on selected pages, plus the explicit whole-document partition:
selected pages `PENDING`; all other pages `OUT_OF_EVALUATION_SCOPE`, never `OTHER`.
This is a review proposal, **not approved Gold**. Open `index.html` to review the original pages and
the complete source IDs/glyph geometry. Every positive and negative needs independent adjudication.
Include document title/subtitle, as separately reviewed units; do not blanket exclude headings in tables.

Before any scoring of real predictions, approve the scope, complete independent source-only review,
and close each Gold unit with a reviewed immediate successor or an independently attested document end.
An extent or relevant boundary outside the review scope requires explicit scope expansion and a new
versioned review, not invented negatives. Never consult treatment interpretations to construct Gold.

## Scorer candidate

`P7EvaluationUniverse` V2 supersedes V1's overly permissive crossing evaluability. Existing V1
receipts are immutable historical evidence, not rebaselined or claimed to verify the new code.
`P7PilotScorer` is tested with synthetic fixtures only, not a real semantic accuracy result.

- Membership is **occurrence-level set** TP/FP/FN; continuation membership is not anchor existence.
- Exact extent is ordered source IDs **and spans**, at a separately adjudicated true anchor.
- Unknown cross-scope extent: preserve full prediction; exact `NOT_EVALUABLE`, not wrong or exact.
- A wrong part entirely within a closed reviewed unit is scoreable. Missing boundary witness remains open.
- False/non-heading anchors are membership diagnostics, outside the true-anchor exact denominator.
- Unknown reference/span is a contract failure, not a favorable exclusion; membership metrics block.
- Overlap and duplicate anchors are counted without pruning. Per-response exact counts are diagnostics,
  not distinct-unit accuracy; duplicates must be resolved by a separately frozen evaluation policy.
- Function `OTHER` never forces an extent cut. Fact validation is not semantic verification.

Source, Gold, scorer and request manifests stay independent. Only source-selection proposal and
scorer candidate are emitted here; Gold and treatment requests remain **NOT_FROZEN**.
Token mapping/usage remain blocked; provider execution and production promotion remain locked.
